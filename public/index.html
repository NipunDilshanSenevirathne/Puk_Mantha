THE SURVIVAL GUIDE

Cooperate. Or catastrophate.

Everyone is on the same raft. Aim in the direction you want to travel, then fart to push the whole crew that way.

  1. PC: WASD or arrows aim; hold Space to fart. Remap keys in Settings. A standard gamepad works too: left stick or D-pad to aim, A / right trigger to fart.
  2. Phone: use the direction pad and hold the FART button. Landscape gives you more room.
  3. Refuel: gas regenerates. Golden beans refill gas and repair the raft.
  4. Survive: rocks and riverbanks damage the raft. Time trial has a deadline. Free sailing removes the clock. Team race splits 2 or 4 players across two rafts; first to finish wins.
  5. Coordinate: agree on a direction. Opposite thrusts cancel each other. Voice chat helps. Allegedly.

Use gentle thrust for tight turns and boost on clear straights. Opposite thrust brakes the raft. Checkpoints repair the raft. All characters have equal power; the job titles are purely terrible jokes.
